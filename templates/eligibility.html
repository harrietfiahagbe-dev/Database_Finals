Eligibility & fees

Short reference for passport validity and fee amounts used in NPIMS.

Who can apply

  • Ghanaian citizens with a valid 9-digit national ID (Ghana Card).
  • One passport application may be pending or processing at a time.
  • Visa applications require an existing issued passport on file.

Passport validity

Ordinary passports are typically issued for up to 10 years. Renew early if expiry is within 90 days - staff reports list passports nearing expiry.
