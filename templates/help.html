Help

Quick answers for citizens and staff using NPIMS.

How do I apply for a passport?

Sign up or log in with your 9-digit Ghana Card number, open Apply for passport, submit the form, then record payment.

Why was my second passport application blocked?

You may only have one passport in pending or processing at a time.

How do I track my application?

Use Track from the main menu. Search by passport or visa ID, or leave the box empty to list all of your records.
